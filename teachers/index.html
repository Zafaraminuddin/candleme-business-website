Four ways forward

Choose what your students need now.

Student Launch Path is not another directory to search through, and it is not a form to fill out. Learn, Advance, Prepare, Achieve: four ways for your students to move toward the help they need. The destinations live inside the candle itself, not on another page of this site.

Learn

Build English, listening and communication confidence, the foundation that makes every other step easier.

Advance

Follow a subject further, from mathematics and science to the ideas that widen your world.

Prepare

Find writing guidance, books, audiobooks and reference tools for the work ahead.

Achieve

Build digital, workplace and everyday skills, then put what you learn into practice.

Why teachers share it

The website explains. The candle connects.

When students need help outside class, the hardest part is often knowing where to begin. Student Launch Path gives them one memorable place to return to for English practice, academic help and practical skills. Each destination opens that provider's own site, and no login is needed to open and explore the candle.

The Keeper door

Tap the CandleMe logo twice.

Once the flame is extinguished, tap the CandleMe logo twice. That takes you directly to the Keeper door, the candle's control point for what comes next.

From there you can Keep, Share, Explore, or open Origin, and move into the other doors and destinations when you need them.

Simple.
Easy to remember.
Easy to share.
How the candle works

Four simple steps.

  1. 01
    Share it

    Give the candle in person, or send it by link, email, text or WhatsApp.

  2. 02
    Open it

    The student opens the lit candle and extinguishes the flame.

  3. 03
    Enter Keeper

    They double-tap the CandleMe logo to open the Keeper door.

  4. 04
    Choose a path

    They open Explore for resources, or use Keep, Share and Origin.

Nothing extra for you to manage
No account or email confirmationNo personal detailsEasy to remember and share
Inside the student's Keeper door

Students choose what they need next.

Once a student opens Keeper, four clear choices keep the experience simple. Keep saves the candle and its story. Share passes it forward. Explore opens the useful destinations. Origin explains where the candle came from.

Teachers do not need to guide every click. Students can return and choose what they need next.
