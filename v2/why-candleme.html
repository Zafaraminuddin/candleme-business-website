Why CandleMe

A link ends at the click. A Candle keeps working.

A saved link takes someone to a page. A Candle is something they keep, come back to, and pass on. The destinations inside can change. The Candle, and the chain it belongs to, stay connected.

What your Candle does for you that a link can’t.

01

Save instantly.

Clients save your Candle without registering, logging in, or sharing personal information.

02

Return easily.

Your booking, shopping, reviews, offers, and recommendations stay together in the client’s Wallet.

03

Grow through sharing.

Every share reaches someone new. See which professionals and locations are creating the most activity.

Businesses lose clients between the visit and the next decision. Booking, shopping, reviews, and loyalty sit in different places. CandleMe brings them together in your Candle and lets clients save it before any registration. That makes your business easier to keep and easier to return to.

Everything together in your Candle, inside your client’s Wallet.

A simple experience for clients. A clear view for you.

Clients see a gift. You see locations, team onboarding, Candles and activity: saves, returns, shares, and which destinations get used. Reports and exports come standard. If you want data flowing into the systems you already run, specific exports, API access, and CRM or POS connections are scoped separately around your setup.
