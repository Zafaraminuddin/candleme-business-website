Why CandleMe

A link ends at the click. A Candle keeps working.

A saved link takes someone to a page. A Candle is something they keep, come back to, and pass on. The destinations inside can change. The Candle, and the chain it belongs to, stay connected.

What a Candle does that a link can’t.

1. It’s kept, not bookmarked.

A Candle lives in the client’s Wallet next to their boarding passes and cards. It’s there when they think of you, not buried in a browser.

2. Every share is a new Candle in a chain.

When a client shares it, the next person receives their own Candle, connected to the one it came from. You can see which Candles bring in new clients. A link that gets copied and pasted tells you nothing.

3. It’s useful before anyone signs up.

Clients never hit a registration wall. They keep the Candle first. If your program ever needs a client to identify themselves, that happens later and only when it matters. The one exception is your own team: an employee enters a name and an email or mobile number once, so the right location is assigned and their Candle can be recovered.

Give it once. The relationship continues.

A simple experience for clients. A clear view for you.

Clients see a gift. You see locations, team onboarding, Candles and activity: saves, returns, shares, and which destinations get used. Reports and exports come standard. If you want data flowing into the systems you already run, specific exports, API access, and CRM or POS connections are scoped separately around your setup.

Privacy by default. Employee contact details are used for activation and recovery only. They are never shown on the Candle, visible to clients, or used for marketing.
