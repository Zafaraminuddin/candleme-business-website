Your CandleMe dashboard.

From setting up locations to seeing who has joined, everything is in one place. Select any screenshot to see it full screen.

Example screens.

01 / 11

Your business at a glance

See views, saves, returns and team activity together. Spot what needs attention and choose where to look next.

02 / 11

Make it your business

Set your business name, logo, website and contact details. Choose who can create Candles for your organization.

03 / 11

Choose what goes inside

Bring booking, products and useful resources into your Candle. Manage destination links and control which automatic suggestions can appear.

04 / 11

See who has joined

Review your team’s accounts, roles, status and Candle assignments in one place. Help people who still need to get started.

To identify who is missing, compare joined accounts with your employee list.

05 / 11

Your Candles, connected

Find and manage your organization’s Candles. Review how they connect and see the selected Candle’s setup and activity.

06 / 11

See the whole structure

Search your managed Candles by name, unit type or status. Open an individual Candle to look more closely.

07 / 11

See where activity happens

Review activity across locations and employee channels. Choose a reporting period, compare the results and export the report.

08 / 11

See where your Candle travels

Explore the countries, regions and cities your Candle reaches. Follow how sharing spreads across generations.

09 / 11

Know what brings people back

Review saves, sharing, return visits and destination feedback. Filter activity by company, location, employee or assigned Candle.

10 / 11

Keep your account in view

Review your subscription, subscribed locations, enabled destinations and transaction history.

This example account has billing disabled. Displayed amounts are test configuration, not a pricing quote.

11 / 11

Connect the systems you already use

Generate personalized Candle links for your mailing platform, CRM or automation tools. Choose the base Candle, personalize the welcome message and track campaign progress.
