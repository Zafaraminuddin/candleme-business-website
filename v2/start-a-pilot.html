What is an activation?

The first time someone intentionally opens a Candle from its QR code or link. During the pilot there is no activation limit and no activation charges.

What are the four groups?

Four organized areas inside the Candle for booking, shopping, reviews, loyalty, offers, and more. All four are open during your pilot at no extra cost.
