Questions salon owners ask

Do clients need an app or an account?

No. They scan, save the Candle to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, and that’s it. Double-tapping the CandleMe symbol brings them straight back to you.

Does this replace my booking software?

No. CandleMe makes the services you already use easier to save and find again. Booking, shopping, loyalty, reviews, gift cards, and more can all sit together inside one Candle in the client’s Wallet.

What happens when a professional leaves?

Their Candle belongs to your business. You can deactivate it, or keep it running so their clients still reach your salon. Your choice.

What if a team member loses their Candle?

They use a private recovery page to find the Candle already assigned to them and save it again. See the Sample Recovery Page.

What does my team have to do?

Scan the location QR once, enter a name and an email or mobile number, and start sharing. About thirty seconds.

How do clients get it?

A professional shows the QR on their phone or sends the link. It can happen in the chair, before or after the service, at checkout, at rebooking, or in a follow-up text.

Will it get lost in the client’s phone?

Wallets are far less crowded than browsers, messages, or social feeds. Marqeta research found that 61% of mobile-wallet users kept only one or two payment cards, so a distinctive Candle can remain among a small number of useful items. Read the research summary.

What does it cost after the pilot?

The standard monthly rate for your plan, per location. Each plan includes a set number of activations a month, and additional activations are billed at your plan’s rate. Full details are on the pricing page.

Can I change what’s inside later?

Yes. Update any destination from your dashboard and clients see the change the next time they open their Candle.

Can my Candle earn money?

Later, yes. Selected destinations can host relevant partners. Ask about it once your pilot has real numbers.
