Why it is different

The Candle stays useful after the first handoff.

The value is not the QR code or the page behind it. The value is a unique Candle that can continue through returns, destinations and onward sharing.

Unique from the start

Each Candle can already have its own ID, QR code, approved destinations, permissions and attribution before the recipient does anything complicated.

Useful after the moment

A recipient can keep the Candle and return later to the live destinations inside it, rather than trying to remember where a link came from.

Shared, not merely copied

When a Candle is passed onward, the next person can receive another unique Candle connected to the path it came from.

Activity stays attributable

Returns, sharing and destination activity can be measured around the Candle. Where connected systems provide the data, additional outcomes can also be attributed under the program rules.

Low friction matters

Participation first. Identity only when the program needs it.

CandleMe does not need to lead every recipient through a registration wall. The Candle can be useful first, while identity is added only when the program actually requires it.

Capability, history and continuity can exist before identity is required.

A saved website preserves access. CandleMe lets an organization pre-configure a distinct, operational Candle that can be kept, revisited and shared without making sign-up the first experience.

Employee onboarding is the exception. Before a company Employee Candle activates, the employee enters their name and either email or full mobile number. This controls location assignment, recovery and duplicate prevention without adding a separate code or OTP step.
